Create a complete, responsive, full-stack Hospital Management Web Application with data persistence using SQLite or PostgreSQL.

TAB 1: MEDICINE INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
- Fields per Medicine: Medicine Name, Batch Number, Dispatch Date, Receive Date, Expiry Date, Quantity in Stock, Default Selling Cost (editable during patient billing), MRP (Maximum Retail Price), Manufacturer/Supplier, Rack/Cabinet Location, and Notes/Description.
- Automated Alerts & Indicators:
  - Visual highlight/warning badges for Low Stock (e.g., quantity < 10).
  - Visual highlight/warning badges for Near-Expiry (within 30/60 days) and Expired items.
- Full CRUD functionality: Add, view, search, filter, edit, and delete medicine records.

TAB 2: PATIENT BILLING & INVOICING
- Generate new patient bills by selecting medicines from inventory (with auto-suggest/search).
- Allow overriding/editing the default selling cost per item during billing without altering the master inventory cost.
- Automatically adjust/deduct inventory quantity when a bill is finalized.
- Include hospital charges (e.g., OPD consultation fees, IPD stay charges, lab/test fees).
- Printable Invoice Layout: Clean, professional, printable invoice view (via browser print / PDF format) including Hospital Details, Patient Name, Date, Itemized List, Subtotal, Discounts, Total Amount, and Signature line.

TAB 3: OPD, IPD & EXTERNAL REFERRALS
- OPD (Outpatient Department):
  - Track OPD patient visits, automatic appointment numbering system (e.g., OPD-1001, OPD-1002), patient details (Name, Age, Gender, Contact, Doctor Consulted, Fee Charged).
- IPD (Inpatient Department / Admissions):
  - Track hospitalized patients: Admission Date & Time, Discharge Date & Time, Assigned Bed/Ward/Room Number, Admitting Doctor/Staff, Reason for Admission, Current Status (Admitted / Discharged).
- External Referrals / Outside Checkups:
  - Track patients sent to outside centers: Patient Name, Referring Doctor, External Lab/Hospital Name, Name of Test/Checkup, Referral Date, Status (Pending / Report Received).

TAB 4: STAFF MANAGEMENT & ATTENDANCE / SALARY CALCULATION
- Staff Directory:
  - Add and manage employees (Name, Designation/Role, Phone, Joining Date, Monthly Base Salary).
- Attendance Tracker:
  - Daily attendance logging (Present, Absent, Half-Day, Paid Leave).
  - Monthly attendance calendar or table view.
- Automated Salary Calculation Rule:
  - Allow 2 free day-offs (leaves) per month without any salary deduction.
  - If total absent days in a month exceed 2 days:
    Calculate salary deduction = (Monthly Base Salary / Days in Month) * (Total Absent Days - 2).
    Net Salary = Monthly Base Salary - Salary Deduction.
  - Generate/Print monthly salary slips or export summary reports.

TAB 5: DATA REPORTS & EXPORTS
- Permanent storage in database (storing all patient, billing, attendance, and inventory records indefinitely).
- Search and filter tools across all tables by date ranges, patient names, or medicine names.
- CSV / Excel export functionality for billing summaries, inventory logs, and staff salary reports.
